Our Origins

The Story Behind Us

IntelliHome was founded by Er. Anjali Verma, MBA (Electronics & Telecommunication Engineer) and Er. Ankit Singh Verma (Computer Science Engineer), bridging a rich 25-year family legacy of residential electrical contracting with the vanguard of software development.

For over two decades, our family business has powered premium commercial hubs and luxury residential estates. By merging this deep domain expertise in precision structured cabling and electrical infrastructure with modern computer science, we created IntelliHome to solve the key challenges of smart homes — transforming complex, fragmented devices into a singular, rock-solid KNX wired home automation network.

Operating out of Nagpur, Maharashtra, IntelliHome serves as the premier smart engineering partner for discerning architects, builders, and luxury homeowners in the region.

By combining custom architectural electrical blueprints, copper comb-bus distribution panels, and advanced DALI circadian lighting networks, we deliver systems engineered to last a lifetime.
