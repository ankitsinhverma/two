Everything you need to know about smart home automation costs, systems, and execution in Nagpur.

The cost of smart home automation in Nagpur depends on system architecture and property scale. Wireless retrofit solutions for a 3BHK typically range from ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000 with zero civil work. Comprehensive wired KNX and DALI automation for luxury bungalows and villas usually ranges from ₹4,50,000 to ₹15,00,000+ depending on circuits, keypads, motorized curtains, and AV integration.

For under-construction villas and full architectural renovations, wired KNX is the gold standard because twisted-pair bus cables are embedded inside walls before plastering, guaranteeing zero cloud dependence and 30-year reliability. For completed homes and occupied apartments, wireless retrofit (Zigbee 3.0 / Matter) is ideal because micro-modules install behind existing switchboards without chipping walls or repainting.
