02

Scope of Engineering Services

IntelliHome provides specialized turnkey smart home engineering services, including:

  • Architectural Consultation & CAD Integration: Reviewing structural drawings to formulate automation schematics, bus topologies, and conduit paths.
  • Certified Wired KNX Automation: Panel engineering, industrial DIN-rail actuator assembly, decentralized fieldbus wiring, and custom ETS programming.
  • DALI Architectural Lighting: Circadian daylight harvesting, DALI broadcast/addressable driver integration, and smooth 0.1% flicker-free dimming.
  • Motorized Curtains & Shading: Precision measurement and installation of whisper-quiet motorized curtain tracks and motorized roller shades.
  • Enterprise WiFi 6E Mesh & VLAN Setup: Multi-gigabit RF heatmap design, ceiling PoE access point placement, and VLAN security segmentation.
  • Distribution Board (DB) Manufacturing: Precision wiring, phase balancing, circuit breaker protection, and surge protection device (SPD) integration.

Quotations, Pricing & Validity

1. Quotation Validity: All written project estimates, Bills of Quantities (BOQs), and quotations issued by IntelliHome remain valid for thirty (30) calendar days from the date of issuance. 2. Scope Variations: Any client-requested alterations to the agreed floor plan (such as adding keypads, changing lighting circuits, or rerouting conduit lines) after quotation approval will be documented in a written Variation Order (VO) and billed separately.

3. Statutory Taxes: Unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing, all commercial quotations are subject to applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) under Indian tax laws.

04

Site Readiness & Client Responsibilities

To ensure flawless execution and avoid electrical hazards, the Client is responsible for:

  • Unimpeded Physical Access: Providing IntelliHome technicians and engineers with safe, unobstructed access to the work site during agreed working hours.
  • Civil Infrastructure Coordination: Ensuring the civil contractor adheres to the conduit piping depths, back-box dimensions, and wall chasing specifications provided in IntelliHome CAD schematics.
  • Electrical Earthing & Neutral Provisions: Delivering a stable 230V AC (±10%) power supply with a true neutral and verified earthing resistance (≤ 2 Ohms) prior to panel energization.
  • Testing Power: Providing uninterrupted temporary utility power during testing, configuration, and commissioning phases.
05

Hardware Procurement & Delivery

Smart control switchplates, capacitive glass panels, touchscreens, actuators, and motorized tracks are procured specifically against approved project milestones. IntelliHome inspects all hardware for quality benchmark compliance prior to on-site delivery.

Once delivered to the client site, the safe custody and dry storage of hardware components against physical theft, water leakage, or site damages caused by civil laborers rests with the Client until final handover.

06

Warranty Standards & Exclusions

IntelliHome stands firmly behind our engineering workmanship and partner hardware:

Component / Service Warranty Duration Coverage Scope
TIS Control & KNX Actuators 2 to 5 Years (Manufacturer) Internal relay, processor, or firmware manufacturing defects.
Designer Touch Keypads 2 Years (Manufacturer) Capacitive glass sensors, OLED/IPS displays, backlighting circuits.
Motorized Curtain Motors 3 to 5 Years (Manufacturer) Internal planetary gearboxes, quiet drive motors, wireless receivers.
IntelliHome Installation & Wiring 1 Year (Workmanship) Panel terminations, cable dressing, scene programming adjustments.

Warranty Exclusions: The above warranties do NOT cover failures resulting from:

  • Severe electrical surges, phase-to-neutral reversals, or lightning strikes that exceed the rating of installed Class II/III Surge Protection Devices.
  • Water infiltration, roof seepage, chemical damage, or rodent/termite damage inside wall conduits or distribution boards.
  • Unauthorized tampering, rewiring, or third-party electrical modifications performed without the prior written consent of IntelliHome engineers.
  • Physical impact, cracked capacitive glass, or scratches incurred following post-installation handover.
07

Payment Milestones & Commercial Terms

Standard turnkey residential automation projects adhere to the following milestone structure:

  • Stage 1 (10% Advance): Project booking, CAD blueprint formulation, and conduit routing schematics.
  • Stage 2 (40% Mobilization): Procurement of field cabling, back-boxes, motorized curtain tracks, and core infrastructure.
  • Stage 3 (40% Hardware Delivery): Dispatch of smart touch panels, DIN-rail automation controllers, and distribution boards.
  • Stage 4 (10% Handover): Final ETS system commissioning, mobile bridge pairing, scene fine-tuning, and client handover.

Invoices are payable within seven (7) business days of milestone achievement. Delays in milestone payments may result in an equivalent adjustment of delivery and installation schedules.

IntelliHome retains an encrypted archival copy of the project database to facilitate future maintenance and repairs.
