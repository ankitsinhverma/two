Frequently Asked Questions

Structured cabling places all network lines, audio/video channels, security wires, and smart bus cables inside concealed PVC conduits before walls are plastered. Once closed, adding new cables requires messy civil work and wall chasing.

Running high-voltage (230V AC) mains cables directly adjacent to data cables induces 50Hz electromagnetic hum, causing network packet loss and audio distortion. We maintain strict physical separation and separate conduit pathways.

Nagpur and the Vidarbha region experience intense lightning and grid voltage transients during monsoon seasons. An SPD installed in your main electrical distribution board clamps dangerous voltage spikes safely to ground before they destroy sensitive smart electronics, TV displays, or inverter boards.

We install 23AWG pure solid copper Cat6A UTP/FTP cables capable of sustained 10-Gigabit Ethernet transmission up to 100 meters, ensuring future-proof bandwidth for years to come.
