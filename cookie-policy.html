What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

A cookie is a small alphanumeric text file stored on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit a website. Cookies allow websites to remember user actions, prevent security attacks, and ensure pages render smoothly.

In addition to HTTP cookies, modern web platforms utilize HTML5 Local Storage. Local storage allows web applications to store key-value data directly in your browser without transmitting it back and forth to a web server on every HTTP request. For instance, your cookie consent preferences and visual theme selections are saved in local storage to keep data transfers to an absolute minimum.

02

Why We Use Cookies

IntelliHome is committed to maintaining a lean digital footprint. We use cookies and local storage tokens strictly for:

  • Core System Operations: Enabling rapid navigation, preserving form states, and guarding against cross-site request forgery.
  • Privacy Preference Persistence: Storing whether you have accepted or declined optional tracking so you are not prompted repeatedly.
  • Performance Optimization: Measuring page load speeds, high-resolution architectural image caching, and mobile responsiveness.
03

Cookie Categories

We classify all storage technologies on our website into three transparent tiers:

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential):

These cookies are indispensable for the website to function properly. They handle core security, session continuity, and store your cookie consent preferences. Under data protection regulations, strictly necessary cookies do not require prior consent because the website cannot operate without them.

2. Analytics & Performance Cookies (Optional):

These allow us to count page visits, evaluate which technical datasheets or product catalogs are most frequently accessed, and identify loading bottlenecks. All data is collected in anonymous, aggregated form without identifying individual visitors.

3. Functional & Experience Cookies (Optional):

These enable enhanced interactive features, such as preserving your dark/light theme choices, remembering layout preferences, and providing smooth motion transitions.

How to Manage Cookies via Web Browsers

In addition to our on-site preference manager, you can configure your web browser to reject, restrict, or delete cookies. Please refer to your browser’s official documentation:

  • Google Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies (Chrome Help Guide)
  • Apple Safari: Settings › Safari › Advanced › Block All Cookies (Safari Help Guide)
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions › Manage and delete cookies (Edge Help Guide)
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data (Firefox Help Guide)
